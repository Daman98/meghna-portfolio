Monitoring & Control

Designing a workforce management platform for data center teams, including journey mapping, dashboard UX, and tools for labor force managers and contributors.

The Problem - Labor Force Managers

7 and more work buckets to manage
10 and more tools & systems involved
0 Unified workforce views
10+ tool tabs opened
0 Automation and manual work discovery & prioritization

Problem solution mapping

design opportunity 01

Create a Unified Work View

Bring ongoing, upcoming, prioritized, assigned, and unassigned work into one centralized dashboard, giving managers a complete picture of their team's workload.

design opportunity 02

Make Work Discoverable & Actionable

Move beyond manual scanning by helping managers surface, filter, sort, and prioritize relevant work based on urgency, status, site, and team needs.

design opportunity 03

Connect People With Work

Combine work demand with workforce availability so managers can quickly understand who is available, who has capacity, and who is best positioned to take on work.

design opportunity 04

Surface Team Progress & Blockers

Provide at-a-glance visibility into technician workload, task progress, blockers, attendance, and team performance, allowing managers to identify where intervention is needed.

design opportunity 05

Reduce Tool Fragmentation

Bring the most important signals from multiple operational systems into one management experience, reducing context switching and manual information gathering.

The Problem - Labor Force Contributor

10 Tools to navigate
0 Unified views of daily work
0 Team & growth visibility
0 Automation, Manual Work & status tracking

Problem solution mapping

design opportunity 01

Create a Personalized Work View

Give contributors a single personalized view of their day—showing assigned tasks, priorities, blockers, urgent issues, and upcoming work so they immediately know what to work on next.

design opportunity 02

Bring Work Into One Experience

Surface critical information from multiple operational systems within one connected workspace, reducing the need to jump between 10+ tools just to understand and complete work.

design opportunity 03

Simplify Work & Status Updates

Reduce repetitive manual effort by making it easier to update task status, record progress, and surface changes within the contributor's existing workflow.

design opportunity 04

Make Team Progress Visible

Design opportunity: Give contributors visibility into team workload, progress, blockers, and relevant peer activity, helping technicians coordinate instead of operating independently.

design opportunity 05

Connect Daily Work to Growth

Design opportunity: Help contributors understand how their completed work connects to performance, skills, professional goals, and development opportunities, creating a clearer path for growth.
